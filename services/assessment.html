The 90 minutes

What happens in the session.

  1. 01

    How a normal day runs

    You walk us through a day, from the first call in the morning to the last job closed out.

  2. 02

    Every system, on screen

    Your team shares screens and shows each piece of software the way it is really used, not the way the manual says.

  3. 03

    Every hand-off and every manual task

    We map where information moves between systems, and where a person moves it by hand.

  4. 04

    Where to start

    We agree which automations would take the most off the desk, and in what order.

You leave with

A map, and a place to start.

  • A map of every system and every hand-off
  • A list of the tasks people do by hand
  • The automations worth building first, in order
  • A phased proposal afterwards, if you want one
Who should be there

The people who run the day.

  • The owner
  • Whoever runs the office
  • Whoever does scheduling or dispatch

The process

Four stops from first call to running.

Nothing changes in your business until you have seen the map and agreed the plan.

  1. 01Week one

    Intro call

    A short call. You tell us how the business runs, what software you use, and where the hours go.

    You getA straight answer on whether we can help.

  2. 0290 minutes

    Operations assessment

    A working session with your team, screens shared. We walk through each system and map every hand-off and every task someone does by hand.

    You getA map of your systems and hand-offs.

  3. 03In phases

    Proposal

    A plan in phases, starting with a data layer over the software you already use, so you can see the business clearly before anything changes.

    You getA phased plan with the scope of each stage.

  4. 04Monthly

    Build and run

    We build the automations, run them for you, watch them, and report on them every month.

    You getAutomations that keep running, and a monthly report.

What we automate

The work between the systems.

Routing

Routes and schedules

Routes balanced across every truck, and rebuilt when a job cancels or runs long.

Estimates

Estimates and follow-ups

Estimates out the same day as the walkthrough, with follow-ups scheduled so nothing waits on someone remembering.

Quality

Quality checks

Technician notes and material usage read on every work order, with the outliers flagged for review.

Performance

Dollar per hour

Field hours matched to job revenue, by crew and by technician, every week.

Payroll

Payroll prep

Hours from your field software and bonuses from your scorecards combined into one sheet, ready for your payroll provider.

Calls

Calls and follow-up

Calls logged to the right customer with the recording linked, and commercial accounts followed up on schedule.
